About us

A neighbourhood barber shop built on good cuts and good conversation.

Est. in Shalley Valley

Your local barber

We opened this shop with one idea: a haircut should be careful, clean and on time. Our barbers have years of experience with classic cuts, modern fades and traditional shaves.
